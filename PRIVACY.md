# Privacy

Your data stays with you. Ask AI uses the Gemini Nano model built into Chrome, so questions and page content are processed inside your browser.

- Your question and the page content never reach a server.
- No accounts, no sign-in, no usage data, no analytics, no tracking.
- The only thing stored is the AI model itself (downloaded once by Chrome) and your most recent question, so the popup remembers it if you reopen.

If that ever stops being true, this file will say so.
